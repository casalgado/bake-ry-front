# Public Authority Data Request Policy

**Effective date:** July 30, 2026
**Owner:** Carlos Alberto Salgado
**Applies to:** All Platform Data received via Meta APIs (WhatsApp Business Messaging), including user phone numbers, message content, profile information, and related metadata.

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how requests from public authorities (e.g., government agencies, law enforcement, regulators) for users' personal data or personal information are handled. It does not cover requests related to search warrants or court orders associated with criminal investigations, which are handled under standard legal process regardless of this policy.

## 2. Required Legality Review

Before any Platform Data is disclosed in response to a request from a public authority, the following must be verified:

- The request comes from an authority with actual jurisdiction over the matter.
- The request cites a valid legal basis (e.g., statute, regulation, or legal process appropriate to the requesting country).
- The request is in writing and identifies the specific data being sought.
- The identity of the requesting authority/individual is verified before any response is prepared.

If any of the above cannot be confirmed, the request is not acted on until it can be, or until qualified legal counsel is consulted.

## 3. Provisions for Challenging Unlawful Requests

If a request fails the legality review in Section 2, or otherwise appears to exceed the requesting authority's legal power:

- No data is disclosed.
- The requesting authority is informed in writing of the specific deficiency (e.g., missing legal basis, incorrect jurisdiction, overbroad scope).
- Where appropriate and available, formal legal channels are used to contest the request (e.g., requesting it be reissued through proper process, or consulting outside counsel on formal objection options).
- The outcome of the challenge is recorded in the Data Request Log (see Section 5).

## 4. Data Minimization

Where a request is determined to be valid:

- Only the specific data fields explicitly requested and legally required are disclosed.
- No bulk or exploratory disclosure is made beyond the scope of the request.
- Where a request is ambiguous or broader than necessary, clarification is sought before disclosing anything, and disclosure is limited to the narrowest reasonable interpretation.

## 5. Documentation

Every request received from a public authority is logged in the Data Request Log, recording at minimum:

- Date the request was received.
- Requesting authority and jurisdiction.
- Legal basis cited.
- Outcome of the legality review.
- Data disclosed (if any) and the legal reasoning for disclosure.
- If challenged: the basis for the challenge and the outcome.
- Who made the decision (name/role).

Logs are retained for at least 3 years and are available for internal review or audit.

## 6. Review

This policy is reviewed annually, and updated as needed to reflect changes in applicable law or in how Platform Data is used.
