# Competitor Landscape Scan: All-in-One Business-Management SaaS for PYMES in Colombia

## TL;DR
- **No single competitor cleanly covers your full scope** (orders + sales + delivery/logistics + accounting + users + balance sheets/financial statements): the market is dominated by accounting/e-invoicing-first suites (Siigo, Alegra, Loggro, World Office, Aliaddo) that nail sales/accounting/financial statements but treat **delivery/last-mile logistics as a white space** — virtually none has native last-mile dispatch.
- **The most dangerous direct competitors are the cloud all-in-one Colombian/LatAm suites** — Siigo (market leader, 300K+ Colombian clients / 1.2M+ LatAm), Alegra (modern challenger), Loggro (15,000+ Colombian businesses), plus World Office, Aliaddo, Siesa and Dataico — because they already bundle sales, accounting, POS, inventory, e-invoicing and financial statements.
- **PYMES actually stitch together point solutions:** an accounting/e-invoice suite + a POS (Vendty, Bsale) + a payments/datáfono (Bold, Nequi Negocios) + a last-mile carrier (Mensajeros Urbanos, Pibox, Rappi). Your "business brain" competes as much against this stitched stack — and against government digital-transformation programs (MinTIC/iNNpulsa CTDE, Bancóldex) — as against any one suite.

## Key Findings
- **Category structure:** The market splits into (a) all-in-one ERP/accounting suites, (b) POS/retail, (c) e-invoicing/DIAN-compliance-first, (d) delivery/logistics, (e) pure accounting/bookkeeping, (f) international players. DIAN e-invoicing is the regulatory wedge that pulled most PYMES into software in the first place.
- **Your differentiator = delivery/logistics.** Native last-mile/dispatch functionality does **not** exist in the mainstream accounting suites. It appears only in restaurant-focused products (Loggro Restobar's *DomiLoggro* own-delivery; Siigo POS Gastrobar's Rappi link) and in Bsale's Chilean courier dispatch panel. Alegra has neither native delivery nor any delivery-app integration in its marketplace. When suites advertise "procesos logísticos," they mean inventory/warehouse, not last-mile.
- **Financial statements (balance sheet / P&L) are table stakes** among accounting-first suites — Siigo, Alegra, Loggro, Aliaddo, ContaPyme, World Office and Helisa all generate *balance general* and *estado de resultados*.
- **Orders + team/user management** are widely covered (multi-user roles; quote→order→invoice flows) by the ERP-grade suites (Loggro, Siigo, World Office, Siesa, Bind).

## Recommendations (where to prioritize deeper research)

**Top 8 TRUE direct / all-in-one competitors (highest scope overlap) — prioritize here:**
1. **Siigo** — the incumbent to beat; broadest PYME footprint + deepest DIAN maturity. Covers everything except delivery.
2. **Alegra** — modern UX, strong PYME/accountant funnel; delivery is a clear gap you can exploit.
3. **Loggro** — closest philosophically ("gestión integral"); already ships native own-delivery (DomiLoggro) in its restaurant line — study it as both competitor *and* proof the delivery module is feasible and valued.
4. **World Office** — dominant for medium companies with complex inventory/production; dated UX = displacement opportunity.
5. **Aliaddo** — all-in-one at aggressive price, 10,000+ clients; overlaps heavily on orders/sales/accounting/users/financials.
6. **Dataico** — e-invoicing-first ecosystem expanding toward all-in-one; watch its module roadmap.
7. **Odoo (with a Colombian DIAN-localization partner)** — the only widely-used platform that genuinely spans accounting + inventory + POS + CRM + delivery/route modules; your most credible "all-in-one incl. logistics" rival.
8. **Siesa (Small Business Suite)** — Advent-backed, deep ERP incl. supply chain; upmarket threat as you scale.

**Staged next steps:**
- **Stage 1 (now):** Deep-dive the top 3 (Siigo, Alegra, Loggro) — pull pricing tiers, module packaging, DIAN certification, and confirm the delivery/logistics gap feature-by-feature. *Threshold that changes the plan:* if ≥2 of them ship native last-mile within the next 2–3 quarters, your delivery differentiator erodes — re-prioritize.
- **Stage 2:** Map the "stitched stack" PYMES actually run (accounting suite + Bold/Nequi + a courier). Your wedge is unifying orders→delivery→accounting in one brain. *Threshold:* validate that target PYMES currently use ≥3 disconnected tools before betting on consolidation as the core value prop.
- **Stage 3:** Engage MinTIC/iNNpulsa CTDE and Bancóldex programs as distribution/subsidy channels rather than competitors. *Threshold:* pursue actively if a program offers co-financing or a certified-vendor listing.
- **Stage 4:** Monitor Odoo partners and Siesa Small Business Suite as your scope expands upmarket.

## Caveats
- Module coverage marks are directional, compiled from vendor sites and Colombian software-comparison portals (comparasoftware, programascontabilidad, guiadesoftware); verify feature-by-feature — vendor marketing overstates "integral."
- Scale figures mix "users," "registered users," "active businesses," and "clients," and some come from third-party reviews or vendor marketing; treat as indicative, not audited. Notably, Alegra's larger regional user counts circulating online are **unverified** (its own 2020 release cited 365K registered users; a 2026 third-party source cites ~150K across CO+MX). Siigo's Colombia figure is 300K+ per its own site; earlier drafts citing ~700K were overstated. Loggro ranges ~11,000–15,000 by source.
- "Delivery/logistics" is the least-covered module and the fastest-moving; restaurant-line delivery (DomiLoggro, Siigo Gastrobar–Rappi) and Bsale's Chile courier panel are the exceptions — confirm current Colombian availability directly.
- Bind ERP is Mexico/SAT-localized and not a true Colombian fit today despite regional marketing.
- Government-program figures/dates span multiple administrations (2016–2025); some initiatives may be renamed or wound down — verify current status.
- Mensajeros Urbanos was acquired by Moova (2023); confirm current branding/operations before treating it as an independent partner or competitor.
